Terms of Service

Last updated: October 27, 2026

By ordering a skin, reshade or thumbnail from me, you agree to these terms. If anything is unclear, ask me on Discord before you order.

1Ordering

Orders are placed by opening a ticket in my Discord server. Your order starts once we've agreed on the details and the price, and payment has been made.

I can turn down any request, including anything hateful, sexual, or that copies another artist's work.

2Payment

  • Payment is 100% upfront, through PayPal or Ko-fi.
  • You cover any transaction fees, so I receive the full agreed price.
  • The final price is set after I review your concept and details, and is agreed before you pay.
  • Rush orders (delivered within 24 hours) cost 2× the normal price.

3Revisions

Small changes are included. Changing the main idea after I've started may cost extra, and I'll tell you the price before making those changes.

4Refunds

  • If you cancel before I start, you get a full refund, minus any transaction fees.
  • Once work has started, payments are not refundable.
  • If I can't finish your order, you get a full refund.

5Delivery and deadlines

All commissions take around 1–7 days, and this can vary depending on queue size. Rush orders are delivered within 24 hours. Skins are delivered as a 64×64 PNG, and thumbnails as a 1280×720 PNG.

6Using your work

Once fully paid, you can use your skin or thumbnail on your channel, servers and socials. You can't resell it, claim you made it, or use it to train AI.

I may show your order in my portfolio and on social media. If you'd like it kept private, tell me when you order.

7Changes to these terms

I may update these terms. Orders follow the terms that were in place when you paid.

8Contact

Questions about these terms? Message me on Discord before ordering.
